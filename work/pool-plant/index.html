What I built

I developed blended learning resources for pool plant operators, combining explanations of water treatment with a virtual tour to support practical training.

How it works

The selected examples use diagrams and numbered hotspots to present equipment in context. Learners can also explore a 360-degree plant-room view, opening information panels about equipment such as a media bed filter.

Delivery

The virtual tour complements preparation for a practical visit and gives learners a way to revisit the setting afterwards. It supports practical training rather than replacing it.
