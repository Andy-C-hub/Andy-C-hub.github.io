The person behind the projects

A little about me.

I’m Andrew, a qualified former teacher turned creative e-learning developer, based in the Midlands, UK. I have a soft spot for a good story and a playful idea. For more than ten years, I’ve used animation, video and visual design to make complex subjects inviting to explore.

That’s taken me from award-winning Disney-inspired resources at the Institute of Swimming to games based on The Inside Man at KnowBe4. I also create photography and illustrations, which bring another side of my creativity into the mix.

I’m now looking for a creative e-learning role where I can help shape an idea, build something engaging and see it through to launch.
